# Elevator Pitch – Kinesiology App 

Our app is a weightlifting and performance tracking platform designed specifically for athletic teams and their coaches. Unlike general workout trackers, the app combines individualized strength progression, team management, and athlete performance analysis in one system. The app uses athlete’s 1-rep max to automatically calculate prescribed training weights using progression equations and stores updated maxes. Coaches can create groups, routines, and exercises, attach instructional video clips/links, and monitor athlete progress through analytical graphs. Athletes can log into workouts, view their progress, and receive recognition when they achieve new PRs. By combining automated calculations, performance data, and team management, the app will provide coaches with better information while giving athletes a simple way to follow and understand their training with a better user interface.  

First, it is built on multiple optimized weight progression equations that are based on athletes’ 1 rep max, offering a deliberate strength training plan.  

Second, unseen in other weight tracking apps, it offers direct support with GymAware velocity bars. This integrates athlete lifting speed to provide lifting plan adaptation if a weight is too challenging or is not challenging enough.  

Third, our app offers an intuitive team/group management and participation system. When signing up, athletes simply enter the group code provided to them. For coaches, they can manage the groups they own, the routines within those groups, and the specific exercises those routines contain. For each exercise, coaches can embed short videos (off YouTube) to help demonstrate the exercise.  

Fourth, our apps offer a more creative and easy-to-use user interface than the previous app used by the client. It will also provide analytical graphs for both students and coaches.  

To directly address our clients’ main point regarding athlete management, our app will handle automatic calculation of athletes’ lifts based on prescribed equations using 1 rep max tests. When the app detects a 1 rep max, it will store that information in the athlete's profile, acting as an automatic base for all future workouts. All lifts per athlete are stored and can be quickly referenced by coaches via analytical graphs and briefings, allowing them to see who is excelling and which athletes may need special attention. Additionally, when an athlete hits a new PR, a notification will be displayed to the other group members as well as the coach. This entire system will be built on a straightforward and easy-to-use interface, creating an intuitive experience.

## Feasibility 
The project is possible within one semester because the application can be broken down into different parts can 4-person team work on different parts parallelly. The core application will use .NET MAUI for the user interface and a cloud-based SQL database for storing athletes, groups, routines, exercises, and workout results. The fundamental functions of athlete registration, group management, workout tracking, and automated weight calculations will be implemented first. Then, more features can be added iteratively, like leaderboards, YouTube workout videos, GymAware integration, notifications, and sophisticated analytics. This enables us to leave more sophisticated features for later sprint goals while maintaining a working application during development.
